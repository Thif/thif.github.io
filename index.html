About

I've spent over a decade at Equinor, first as a reservoir engineer and then as a data scientist. My work covers reservoir characterisation, fluid property prediction, and building machine learning models on subsurface data.

I also work on renewable energy problems: wind power forecasting, wind farm layout optimisation, and solar plant performance. These are areas I've explored both at work and through competitions like the NORA WindAI Hackathon, which I won in 2025.

I take part in Shell.ai Hackathons every year and do Advent of Code puzzles to keep my problem-solving sharp.
